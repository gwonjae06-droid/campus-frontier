# Army composition update

Four-class recruitment is available from the bottom-bar army composition button. Infantry quick recruitment remains available. Cavalry unlocks at military tier 2 for 9 OP; siege unlocks at tier 3 for 10 OP. The panel explains roles, statistics, costs and locks. New recruits still act next round.

Deterministic counters: infantry versus cavalry +2 damage; cavalry versus scout +2; scout versus siege +2; siege versus bases +3. Exact previews include the bonus. AI uses the same damage rules, prioritizes lethal attacks, and recruits an unlocked siege unit and cavalry unit when affordable before falling back to the existing mix.

Save version and fields are unchanged. New balance applies to resumed games too. Full existing suite and new matchup/preview/recruitment/save-roundtrip tests pass. Mobile physical-device FPS and long-run balance are unmeasured. Local browser verification was blocked by browser network suspension during this update.
